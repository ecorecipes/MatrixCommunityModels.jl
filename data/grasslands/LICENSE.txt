The files in this directory hold the fitted vital-rate parameters of the multispecies integral projection models
of five western North American grasslands (Arizona, Idaho, Kansas, Montana, New Mexico), as used by

    Tredennick, A.T., de Mazancourt, C., Loreau, M. & Adler, P.B. (2017). Environmental responses, not species
    interactions, determine synchrony of dominant species in semiarid grasslands. Ecology 98, 971-981.
    https://doi.org/10.1002/ecy.1757

whose models follow

    Chu, C. & Adler, P.B. (2015). Large niche differences emerge at the recruitment stage to stabilize grassland
    coexistence. Ecological Monographs 85, 373-392. https://doi.org/10.1890/14-1741.1

They were written out at full precision by test/reference/R/run_grassland_reference.R from the repository
https://github.com/atredennick/community_synchrony (commit d01b584, 10 March 2017):

- species.csv: for each site and species, the mesh (cells, largest genet in cm², as set in
  runscripts/run_ipm_simulations.R), the intercept, slope, crowding coefficients and spatial scales (alpha) of
  growth and survival, the variance parameters of growth, the mean recruitment intercept and recruitment
  crowding coefficients (crowding_j: the effect of species j on the row species), from results/
  growth_params_list.RDS, surv_params_list.RDS and recruit_parameters.RDS as formatted by the repository's
  R/import_regression_params_fxns.R; and the mean and variance of the log area of recruits, computed from
  data/<site>/<species>/recSize.csv as that file's format_recruitment_params computes them.
- years.csv: the year effects of growth, survival and recruitment, by site, species and year.

The parameters were fitted by the authors to the mapped-quadrat data of the five sites, deposited by Chu & Adler
on Dryad (https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.37n6f) under CC0 1.0. The Dryad files themselves could not be
downloaded (the file requires a login) and were not used.
