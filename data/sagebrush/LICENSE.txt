The files in this directory come from the R package IPMdoit by Andrew Tredennick and Peter Adler,
https://github.com/atredennick/IPMdoit (commit 94124a4e7659ece6c32e75a0cd5991b54f0498c9, 10 September
2015), directory inst/extdata:

- Growth_params_*.csv and Surv_params_*.csv are copied unchanged.
- recruitment.csv and recruitment_years.csv were extracted from recruit_params_GnoG.csv (the posterior
  means of intcpt.mu, intcpt.yr and dd, the last transposed so that crowding_J in the row of species I is
  the effect of J on I, as IPMdoit uses it) and from recSize_*.csv (the mean and variance of the logarithm
  of recruit area), by test/reference/R/run_sagebrush_reference.R.
