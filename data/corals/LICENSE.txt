kayal2018_table_s1.csv holds the parameter estimates of Table S1 of the Supporting Information of

    Kayal, M., Lenihan, H.S., Brooks, A.J., Holbrook, S.J., Schmitt, R.J. & Kendall, B.E. (2018). Predicting coral
    community recovery using multi-species population dynamics models. Ecology Letters 21, 1790-1799.
    https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13153

typed by hand from the copy of the Supporting Information on eScholarship
(https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7nm875tr, file Kayal_etal_Appendices.pdf): for each reef (LTER 1, 2, 4, 5) and
taxon (Acropora, Pocillopora, Porites), the intercept and slope of recruitment, survival and growth, and the
standard deviation of growth. They are published numbers (facts), cited here; no file of the authors was copied.

The authors' R code (https://github.com/MohsenKayal/Coral-assemblage-IPM, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.573772) carries
no licence and is not included in this package. test/reference/R/run_coral_reference.R reads it at run time from a
path given on its command line.

mcr_lter_observed_colonies.csv holds the coral colonies observed on the fore reefs of LTER 1, 2, 4 and 5 in 2013 and
2015, binned by size. It is derived from

    Moorea Coral Reef LTER, Lenihan, H., Ake, H. & Winslow, E. (2022). MCR LTER: Coral Reef: Long-Term Coral Population
    and Community Dynamics: Annual Island Wide Coral Demography Survey 2011 ongoing. Version 5. Environmental Data
    Initiative. https://doi.org/10.6073/pasta/322a76c6f197bfac60c91d66a3e96fb5
    (EDI package knb-lter-mcr.4009, revision 5; table "Mapped Corals long format",
    MCR_LTER_Lenihan_mapped_corals_20220413.csv, 2,300,028 bytes, SHA-1 cb91e282e52f84ea47085431bed6aa6be4274c76)

which its metadata (intellectualRights) releases "under the Creative Commons license Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0, see https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)". The derived file is distributed under the same licence,
CC BY 4.0. The data package asks its users to cite it and to notify its creators or data contact of products that use it.
Paper citation of an earlier revision of the same package: Lenihan, H.S. & Kayal, M. (2015),
doi:10.6073/pasta/98855475790bfb012066f9dc802ecc31.

Changes made (by bin_observed_colonies.jl in this directory, which reproduces the file from the table above):
  - kept only the fore reef (Habitat OR), the years 2013 and 2015, the genera Acropora, Pocillopora and Porites, and
    colonies with length, width and height all recorded in that year;
  - dropped 5 Pocillopora and Porites colonies recorded beyond the first metre of their transect (Acropora was mapped
    over the whole of each 5 m x 1 m transect, Pocillopora and Porites over its first metre);
  - replaced each colony's dimensions by its surface area S (cm²), as in Appendix S2 of Kayal et al. (2018): a sphere
    for Pocillopora, a cylinder for Acropora and Porites;
  - binned z = log10(S + 1) into 100 cells of width 0.07 on [0, 7] and counted the colonies in each cell, by reef,
    year and genus; all other columns (positions, observers, notes, demographic flags) were dropped;
  - added the area over which each genus was mapped on a reef (20 m² for Acropora, 4 m² for the other two).
The tagged corals of 2011-2013 behind the estimates of Table S1 are not included.
