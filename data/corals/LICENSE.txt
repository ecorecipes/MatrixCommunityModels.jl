kayal2018_table_s1.csv holds the parameter estimates of Table S1 of the Supporting Information of

    Kayal, M., Lenihan, H.S., Brooks, A.J., Holbrook, S.J., Schmitt, R.J. & Kendall, B.E. (2018). Predicting coral
    community recovery using multi-species population dynamics models. Ecology Letters 21, 1790-1799.
    https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.13153

typed by hand from the copy of the Supporting Information on eScholarship
(https://escholarship.org/uc/item/7nm875tr, file Kayal_etal_Appendices.pdf): for each reef (LTER 1, 2, 4, 5) and
taxon (Acropora, Pocillopora, Porites), the intercept and slope of recruitment, survival and growth, and the
standard deviation of growth. They are published numbers (facts), cited here; no file of the authors was copied.

The authors' R code (https://github.com/MohsenKayal/Coral-assemblage-IPM, Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.573772) carries
no licence and is not included in this package. test/reference/R/run_coral_reference.R reads it at run time from a
path given on its command line.

The field data behind the estimates (MCR LTER, Environmental Data Initiative) are not included.
