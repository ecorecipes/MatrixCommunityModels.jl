The files in this directory are posterior means and data summaries derived from the dataset

    Gibson, D., Arnold, T., Buderman, F. & Koons, D. (2024). Explaining the divergence of population trajectories
    for two interacting waterfowl species. Dataset, Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.hqbzkh1n9
    (version of 2024-11-19)

which is released under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The files here are
likewise dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. The dataset accompanies

    Gibson, D., Arnold, T.W., Buderman, F.E. & Koons, D.N. (2025). Explaining the divergence of population
    trajectories for two interacting waterfowl species. Ecological Monographs 95, e1642.
    https://doi.org/10.1002/ecm.1642

They were written by test/reference/R/run_waterfowl_reference.R from estimated_vital_rates.rdata (8000 posterior
draws of the integrated population model) and model_files.rdata (the model's data). Neither file is shipped. The
changes made:

- vital_rates.csv: for each species and year 1961-2020, the mean over the 8000 draws of fecundity, natural
  winter survival (s.winter_*), direct harvest probability (kappa_*) and natural summer survival (s.summer_*).
- abundance.csv: for each species and year 1961-2021, the mean over the draws of the breeding numbers (millions)
  of yearling (njf, njm) and older (naf, nam) females and males, and the 5% and 95% quantiles of their total.
- parameters.csv: omega (breeding success of yearling relative to older females) and indirect_harvest (the ratio
  exp(beta.indirect) of the indirect to the direct harvest hazard of adults), which the draws do not store: for
  each draw they were recovered from the draw's abundances in 1961 and 1962 through the process equations of
  IPM_code.R, and their means over the draws are given; bp_ltre, the mean of bp[, 1:8000] as LTRE_Code.R uses it;
  and, from model_files.rdata, the means and standard deviations (mu_BD, sd_BD, mu_HD, sd_HD) by which the model
  standardised the breeding female numbers and the canvasback-to-redhead ratio.
- covariates.csv: from model_files.rdata, the yearly breeding female numbers (mean.BD) and canvasback-to-redhead
  female ratio (mean.HD) that the model took as data, and the breeding population survey estimates (y; none in
  2020 and 2021).

The slopes of fecundity on conspecific female density and on the canvasback-to-redhead ratio used by the feedback
extension (src/waterfowl.jl) are the posterior means printed in Appendix S1, Table S6, of the paper.
