API reference

MatrixCommunityModels.MatrixCommunityModels — Module
MatrixCommunityModels

Matrix community models (Lytle & Tonkin 2023) built by composition: each species is an open stage-structured model, each shared density pool (space, biomass, ...) is a junction of an undirected wiring diagram, and a community is the composite.

source
MatrixCommunityModels.RIPARIAN_GUILDS — Constant
RIPARIAN_GUILDS

Parameters of the five riparian guilds of Lytle et al. (2017): cottonwood C, tamarisk T, willow W, sagebrush S, meadow M. den is stem density per stage (per m²), a the maturation probabilities, S and D the flood and drought mortalities, stasis the stages with a stasis term in the original projection matrix, and area the initial area per stage (m²).

MatrixCommunityModels.MatrixSpecies — Type
MatrixSpecies(name, stages, matrices; occupancy = [], recruitment = [], threshold = nothing)

A species whose survival and fecundity matrices are returned whole by matrices(ctx) -> (U, F). Use it when the matrices come from elsewhere, for example a discretised integral projection model, and listing every entry as a named Transition would add nothing. The keyword arguments are those of Species.

MatrixCommunityModels.OpenCommunity — Type
OpenCommunity

A community with exposed pools. It has a state layout (one block of stages per species), ports (pool names), a readout giving its contribution to each pool, and an update taking the pool totals. Build one from a Species with open_community, or from several with community or compose.

source
MatrixCommunityModels.Species — Type
Species(name, stages, transitions; occupancy = [], recruitment = [], threshold = nothing)

A stage-structured species whose vital rates may depend on shared pools and the environment.

  • occupancy: pool => weights pairs saying how much of each pool the species occupies. weights is a vector (one weight per stage), or a function (n, env) -> Real.
  • recruitment: stage => f pairs; f(ctx) individuals are added to stage each step, independently of the species' own abundance.
  • threshold: an optional map applied to the new stage vector (for example quasi-extinction).

One step is threshold(A(ctx) * n + r(ctx)) with A = U + F.

source
MatrixCommunityModels.Transition — Type
Transition(name, from, to, kind, rate)

One named entry of a projection matrix: individuals of stage from contribute to stage to at rate. kind is :survival (goes into U) or :fecundity (goes into F). rate is a number or a function of a Context.

MatrixCommunityModels.abundance_sensitivity — Method
abundance_sensitivity(build, values, envs; burnin = 0, replicates = 1)

The simulation-based sensitivity of Lytle & Tonkin (2023). build(v) returns a model (a NamedTuple with community and n0) in which one vital rate has the value v. For each v in values the model is projected and the mean abundance of every state after burnin is recorded, averaged over replicates runs. envs is a sequence of environments, or a function r -> sequence giving the sequence for replicate r (use the same sequences for every v to remove sampling noise from the comparison).

Each state's mean abundance is then regressed on values. Returns (slope, r2, means): the slope and R² for each state (in layout order) and the matrix of mean abundances (one row per value). The slope estimates the derivative of mean abundance with respect to the vital rate; R² says how consistent the response is across the range.

MatrixCommunityModels.community_matrix — Method
community_matrix(c, n, env)

The block-diagonal projection matrix of a closed community frozen at state n and environment env, one block per species in layout order. Pool totals are those of n. Only communities whose species all share the top-level pools are supported.

source
MatrixCommunityModels.compose — Method
compose(diagram, components)

Compose open communities (or species) along an undirected wiring diagram. Box b is filled by components[b], whose ports are matched to the box's ports in order. The total of each junction is the sum, in port order, of the readouts attached to it; every component is then updated with the totals of its junctions, all from the same time-t state. Outer ports of the diagram become the pools of the composite.

MatrixCommunityModels.cypripedium_model — Method
cypripedium_model(; equivalence = true, integeronly = false, dir = joinpath(DATA_DIR, "cypripedium"))

The two-orchid community of the adapt3 community-projection vignette (Shefferson 2026): Cypripedium candidum (5 annual matrices) and C. parviflorum (17), nine stages each, cycled through in order. The species are linked by one pool, density, the number of individuals of both species, not counting dormant seed when equivalence is true. Reproduction into dormant seed (SD) and protocorms (P1) is multiplied by Ricker terms 0.5 exp(-density) and 1.2 exp(-2 density). With integeronly the stage vectors are floored each step, as in adapt3.

The annual matrices were estimated in R with lefko3 and are shipped as data. Returns (community, n0, envs); the environment of step t is the integer t - 1, which selects each species' matrix for that year.

MatrixCommunityModels.equilibrium — Method
equilibrium(c, n0, env; maxiter = 100_000, tol = 1e-10)

Iterate a closed community in a constant environment env from n0 until the largest change in any state, relative to max(|n|, 1), falls below tol. Returns (n, converged, iterations). A stable equilibrium is found only if the iteration converges to one; cycles and chaos return converged = false.

MatrixCommunityModels.invasion_growth_rate — Method
invasion_growth_rate(invader, pool, env)
invasion_growth_rate(invader, resident, n, env)

Asymptotic growth rate of a rare invader (a species) when the pools are held at the totals pool (a NamedTuple), or at the totals of the resident community in state n. A value above one means the invader increases when rare. The invader's own contribution to the pools is neglected, as is any dependence of its rates on its own abundance.

MatrixCommunityModels.jacobian — Method
jacobian(c, n, env; h = 1e-6)

Jacobian of one time step of a closed community at state n, by central differences with step h * max(|n_j|, 1). Unlike community_matrix, which freezes the pools, this includes the effect of each state on every species through the pools, so its off-diagonal blocks are the direct between-species effects. At an equilibrium, the equilibrium is locally stable if every eigenvalue has modulus below one.

MatrixCommunityModels.riparian_model — Method
riparian_model(; corrected = false, guilds = RIPARIAN_GUILDS, path = ...)

The five-guild riparian vegetation community of Lytle et al. (2017). Guilds are linked by one pool, space: the area occupied after the year's flood and drought mortality, in cottonwood-seedling units. Recruitment of every guild is proportional to the free space K - space.

With corrected = false the model reproduces the published R script exactly, including two behaviours that look unintended: the meadow guild receives the willow guild's recruitment (expressed here as a second pool, willow_adults), and sagebrush and meadow lose the stage-5 individuals that do not mature. corrected = true gives meadow its own recruitment, gated on its own adults, and keeps those stage-5 individuals. Both variants keep the script's floor of 10 individuals per sagebrush stage.

guilds supplies the parameters; pass a copy changed with with_rate to perturb a vital rate.

Returns (community, n0, envs, K); envs holds one environment per year of the 1916-1998 Yampa River record at Maybell.

MatrixCommunityModels.verde_fish_model — Method
verde_fish_model(; K = 47660.0, dir = joinpath(DATA_DIR, "verde_fish"))

The seven-species Verde River fish community of Rogosch et al. (2019): three-stage biomass models linked by one pool, total community biomass, which limits every species' fecundity through max(0, 1 - biomass / K).

Returns (community, n0, envs, years, density): the composed community, the initial biomass (the stage-3 values of the vital-rate table), one environment per year of the 1964-2017 flow record (year-type flags), the years, and the individuals-per-gram conversion for each species' stages.
